{% comment %} Reusable "torn paper" section-start divider. Three copies of the same jagged silhouette, offset vertically and layered at increasing opacity, simulate stacked sheets of paper — the back two peek out below the front layer's peaks. The front layer carries a grain texture (SVG feTurbulence, clipped to the shape's own alpha) so it doesn't read as flat vector color. The elevation shadow is applied as a CSS drop-shadow on the whole
