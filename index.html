About Us

Aistear — the Gaelic word for "journey" — profoundly reflects our core value of lifelong learning.

We are rooted in an Islamic worldview and committed to nurturing each child's unique fitrah in a space filled with creativity, curiosity, and companionship. We aim to support every learner's individual growth by helping them strengthen their bond with Allah (SWT) and flourish as future leaders who love and live the Quran with excellence (Ihsan).
