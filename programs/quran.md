<p>Our Quran stream brings together two pathways. <strong>Tarteel</strong> is our dedicated Hifdh (memorization) program, built on the Mauritanian Method: a structured approach that pairs steady memorization with loah (slate) writing and reflective tafseer, so children don't just memorize the Quran but grow to understand and connect with it. <strong>Tasdeed</strong> builds the essential foundations every child needs: confident Nazra (Quran reading) and a clear grounding in core Islamic beliefs and practices, delivered at a pace suited to younger learners.</p>
